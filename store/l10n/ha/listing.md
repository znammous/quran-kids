### Description
Nur Al-Wahy manhaja ce kyauta don haddar Alƙur’ani Mai Girma, ga yara da dukan iyali. Yaro yana haddace sashe-sashe kai tsaye a kan ainihin shafin Mushafin Madina, da rubutun Usmani da tsarin shafi irin na Mushafin da aka buga.

HADDACE A MATAKAI 5
Kowane sashe yana da matakai biyar:
• Karanta ka maimaita
• Jera kalmomin aya
• Kalmar da ta ɓace
• Jera ayoyi bisa tsarinsu
• Karanta daga ka: ga mai saurare a gefenka, ko ka naɗa muryarka sannan ka saurare ta

SAURARI SHAHARARRUN MAKARANTA
• Kusan makaranta 20, ciki har da Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi (karatun koyarwa ga yara), Alafasy da As-Sudais
• Maimaita aya ko shafi sau nawa kake so
• Yana aiki ba tare da intanet ba bayan saurare na farko

BITA A LOKACINTA
• Jadawalin bita yana dawo maka da abin da ka haddace a lokacinsa: gobe, bayan kwana 3, bayan mako ɗaya…
• "Gwada kaina", jarrabawar Izu da ƙalubalen yau

TSARE-TSARE A SHIRYE
• Tsare-tsaren manhajar: rubu’in shafi, rabin shafi, shafi guda, ko ayoyi 3 zuwa 8 a kowane sashe
• Tsare-tsaren "Bil-Quran Nahya"
• Ka riga ka haddace wani abu? Shigar da shi, ka samu wirdin bita na kowace rana

KUSURWAR KASADA
• Wasanni 3 da ke amfani da ayoyin da ka haddace: Mai Gudun Ayoyi, Kantar Ayoyi da Tsuntsun Ayoyi
• Gina masallacinka gunda-gunda yayin da kake haddace da bita
• Duwatsu masu daraja, lambobin yabo, shago, da kyautai tsakanin ’yan’uwa
• Kowane ɗan iyali yana da hotonsa (avatar)

FAHIMCI ABIN DA KAKE HADDACE
• Fassarar ma’anonin kowace aya da Hausa (Abubakar Mahmud Gumi) a cikin allon "Ma’anoni"
• Mushafi da ayoyi suna kasancewa da Larabci koyaushe

GA DUKAN IYALI
• Asusu ga kowane ɗan iyali a kan na’ura ɗaya
• "Allon gida" da ke ƙarfafa ’yan’uwa, ba tare da gasa da baƙi ba
• Takardun shaidar haddace da za ka iya rabawa ko bugawa
• Harsuna 14 na manhaja

SIRRINKA NA FARKO
• Babu asusu, babu talla, babu sayayya, babu tattara bayanai
• Ci gabanka yana nan a na’urarka
• Ana sauraron naɗaɗɗun murya sannan a goge su
• Yana aiki ba tare da intanet ba

KYAUTA GABA ƊAYA
Nur Al-Wahy wakafi ce don Allah, sadaka mai gudana. Babu biyan kuɗi ko kaɗan.

Sauke yanzu, ka fara tafiyar haddar Alƙur’ani ta ɗanka, sura bayan sura.
