### Description
Nur Al-Wahy is a free Quran memorization app for children and the whole family. Your child memorizes on the real Madinah Mushaf page, section by section, then reviews it on time so it stays. No ads, no accounts, no purchases.

MEMORIZE ON THE REAL MUSHAF PAGE
• The Madinah Mushaf page in Uthmani script, with the same layout as the printed Mushaf, so the child memorizes the page as it looks.
• Your section is highlighted on the page, and every section goes through 5 stages:
• Read and repeat
• Order the words
• Missing word
• Order the ayahs
• Recite from memory: to a parent who listens, or record yourself and listen back with the text in front of you.

LISTEN TO FAMOUS RECITERS
• About 20 reciters, including Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi (teaching recitation for children), Alafasy and As-Sudais.
• Repeat the ayah or the page as many times as you like.
• Works offline after the first listen.

REVIEW THAT KEEPS WHAT YOU MEMORIZED
• A spaced review schedule brings each section back on time: tomorrow, in 3 days, in a week, and onward.
• "Test myself", Juz tests and a daily challenge.
• Already memorized some surahs? Record what you know and get a daily revision portion.

READY-MADE PLANS
• The app's own plans: a quarter page, half a page, a page, or 3 to 8 ayahs per section.
• The "Bil-Quran Nahya" plans.

ADVENTURE CORNER
• 3 games played with the ayahs you memorized: Ayah Runner, Ayah Shelf and Ayah Bird.
• Build your own mosque piece by piece as you memorize and review.
• Gems, badges, a store, gifts between siblings, and an avatar for each family member.

UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU MEMORIZE
• The translation of the meanings of every ayah in English (Rowwad Translation Center), in the "Meanings" panel.
• The Mushaf and the ayahs always stay in Arabic.

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• A separate account for each family member on the same device.
• A "Home board" that encourages siblings, with no competition against strangers.
• Certificates you can share or print when a surah is complete.
• 14 interface languages.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No accounts, no ads, no purchases, no data collection.
• Progress stays on your device.
• Voice recordings are heard, then erased.
• Works offline.

Nur Al-Wahy is free, offered as a waqf (ongoing charity). We ask Allah to make it a light for every home that memorizes His Book.
